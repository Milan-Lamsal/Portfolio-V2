CASE STUDY 01 / FULL-STACK / REAL-WORLD PRODUCT

Hair by Zoe.

A stylist’s digital home. From discovering a new look to finding a place in the appointment book.

ReactTypeScriptCloudflare D1

hair by zoe / a little self-care starts here
ACTUAL SITE CAPTURE
Implementation documented in project records
01

The problem

A salon website needs to support two different journeys: a visitor exploring a stylist’s work, and a stylist managing appointments behind the scenes.

02

The objective

Bring services, real client photography, appointment availability, and day-to-day administration into one coherent product.

03

The approach

The public experience centers on services, booking, a filterable gallery, and contact. A separate administrative workspace handles bookings, services, clients, photos, and settings.

04

Technical implementation

React and TypeScript run on a Vinext/Vite foundation, with Cloudflare Workers, a D1 SQLite database, and Drizzle. Email notifications use a delivery outbox with per-recipient retry handling. Calendar access is a read-only subscription.

05

Challenges & tradeoffs

Booking state and email delivery are separate concerns: an appointment should not disappear because a notification needs a retry. Responsive WebP images balance the importance of salon photography with page weight.

06

The result

Project records document a deployed booking platform, an administrative workspace, and a gallery with 27 real client looks. Payments are not part of this implementation. The image shown here is an actual saved site capture.

07

Engineering takeaway

A useful full-stack product includes the quieter operational details—delivery retries, manageable content, and clear appointment state—as well as its public interface.
